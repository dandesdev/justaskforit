# Daniel Rangel Guedes Bio

Daniel is someone very interested in technology, science, society, arts and education. Living in São Paulo, Brazil, he has worked as a teacher (Physics, Math, and English to brazilian speakers), as an amateur painter, as a freelancer frontend developer, an educational content creator and as an evaluations editor. 
He's born is a small-medium sized city called Guaratinguetá. At the age of 18 he moved to Campinas to styudy at UNICAMP, where he got his Bachelelor degree in Physics. Then, he moved to the capital São Paulo where he lives to this day.
As a child, he used to doodle and draw whenever he went, making a scene when his parents forgot to bring any white paper and at least a pencil.
His family was in the school business, as his grandmother started a schoolherself, bringing most of the family to work with her. Daniel then had the priviledge to be exposed to high quality education, and got very excited about science, history, philosophy, and also technology. He used to "attend" programming and development classes as an spectator, asking his father, who was working at the school, to let him stay at the room and follow along with the atual, much older students.
Connecting his passions, he started to make animated and interactive websites or flash executables that presented or talked about educational content, which he got thorugh hard physicial books and encyclopedies and the beginning of internet search engines, such as "cadê", "aonde", "yahoo" and then "google". He would then give these educational presentations or minigames to some of his colleagues, to help them understand concepts seen at class. He was a nerd, on a good sense. Loved to study, that's it.
Being an only child and having only a couple of friends at time, which were all from school, and living in a small town, Daniel spent his afternoons bored after he got tired from drawing, playing legos, or searching for something to build or to experiment with in the house, and getting his first computer was a real shift. He then started asking for games, Age Of Empires (from 1 to 4), Age of Mythology, Rise of Nations and other similar ones, which he obscessed with for weeks until he got tired and paused it for a moment. 
Jumping to his late teenager years, Daniel got interested in reading philosophy, history and the impacts of science as a whole on society, also technology. He likes to say technology evolved way quickier than human society, and worries about the problems it's causing with behaviours such as doom scrooling, the consumer culture and the concentration of money and power in giant tech companies. He went to study Physics beacuse he romanticaly and tragically thought the goverment would be more than happy to provide him with a very good life and very high tech lab, however he wanted, for him to study whatever he wanted. Moving to a big city and realizing that capitalism had more power over education and the science job market as he ever thought, he gave up on the idea of just being a Physicist, because, again, he wanted to study whatever he wanted, and sometimes he would like to study Math, other times he liked to read about the human mind, or urbanism, or history, or anything. 
So after undergraduating, just when everything was right for a master's degree, he decided he was tired of only physics for a bit and moved to São Paulo, started giving private lessons in Math and Physics to whoever he got in order to make some money, while he did oil painting, and even sold some. He also wrote a short poetry book called "Gastritologia" - don't tell anyone, only if shows real deep interest in Daniel's life though. The class and tutoring got big, and he got a position at the big school in São Paulo, called Objetivo, to work as a "plantonista", someone who helped students, answering the question they brought. He officially helped with Math and Physics, but also secretly with English. Paralell to that, he sometimes kept studying programming and development, and for some years got really interesting in code art, creating also really interesting science simulations, bring a lot of his passions together again. Already confident in Adobe sofware, remember he started learning Flash when it was Macromedia's, he got very excited about After Effects and its scripts, and started an educational instagram account, where he posted really carefully created educational videos made on After Effects, sometimes using skecthes rendered in Processing, and sometimes doing motion design.
